The hospital continuously receives new patients and needs to treat
 the highest-severity patient immediately.

A Max Heap-based Priority Queue is suitable for this requirement.
For example:
Max Heap:

          90
        /    \
      72      85
     /  \    /  \
   45   65  30   50

The highest-priority patient, 90, is always at the root.
When a new patient arrives, their severity score can be inserted in:

`O(log n)`

The highest-priority patient can be accessed in:

`O(1)`

When the highest-priority patient is treated and removed, the heap can be restored in:

`O(log n)`

Therefore, the Max Heap Priority Queue is appropriate for continuously inserting
 patients while maintaining immediate access to the highest-priority patient.
