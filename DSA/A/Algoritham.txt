1. Algorithm
    1. Start with an empty Max Heap.
    2. Insert the new patient severity score at the end of the heap.
    3. Compare the inserted value with its parent.
    4. If the inserted value is greater than its parent, swap them.
    5. Continue moving the value upward until the Max Heap property is restored.
    6. Repeat for all patient severity scores.
    7. Display the heap after every insertion.
